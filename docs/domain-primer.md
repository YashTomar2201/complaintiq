# Domain Primer (Phase 0 reading)

**CFPB** — Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, a US agency. Consumers file complaints about financial companies; the CFPB forwards them, the company responds, and the complaint is published in a public database (with the consumer's narrative only if they consent).

**Triage** — sorting complaints by urgency/type so the serious ones (money lost, vulnerable customers, legal risk) are handled first.

**Regulations**
- **Regulation E (12 CFR 1005)** — electronic fund transfers: unauthorized debit/ACH transactions, bank's duty to investigate errors.
- **Regulation Z (12 CFR 1026)** — Truth in Lending: credit cards/loans, billing-error resolution (§1026.13), disclosures.
- **FCRA / Regulation V (12 CFR 1022)** — accuracy and privacy of credit reports; dispute rights.
- **FDCPA / Regulation F (12 CFR 1006)** — how debt collectors may contact and treat consumers.

**Key terms** — *timely response*: company answered within the deadline (15 days; up to 60 for closure). *Systemic issue*: many complaints from one root cause. *Human-in-the-loop*: AI drafts, a person approves.
